Learning collection
3,500 frequency-selected lexical entries. Short Chinese and English study
glosses have been selected, reworded and translated from the sources below.
This educational dataset, including the derivative entries and newly written
study phrases, is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
An independent Lao teacher has not checked every entry. Alternative
orthographies are included as search aliases where identified.

Wiktionary (contributors to the English and Chinese Wiktionaries)
The contributors of each entry and its revision history are linked through the
per-entry detail page; append ?action=history to a Wiktionary entry's URL
to see contributors. Lexical data and Vientiane IPA extracted with Wiktextract
via Kaikki, edited / translated for this collection.
https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Main_Page
https://zh.wiktionary.org/
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Lao/
https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/%E8%80%81%E6%92%BE%E8%AA%9E/index.html
https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Word details expose a link to the corresponding source spelling if available.

Lao-English Dictionary by the E-Government Center, Ministry of Posts and
Telecommunications of Laos. English glosses were selected and revised.
The original romanization is shown where a single usable reading was available.
Full MIT license below.
https://github.com/google/language-resources/tree/master/third_party/lo_dictionary_by_mopt_laos

Unicode Unilex, Lao frequency data
https://github.com/unicode-org/unilex/blob/main/data/frequency/lo.txt
Source snapshot e20a4f446ed42df7df25782e102bd13f218a88aa
4,364,221 corpus tokens; normalized frequencies are per billion. NFC/NFKC,
explicit tone-order and common historical-spelling equivalences were used to
combine readings. Fragments and entries without a defensible meaning were
excluded. Ranking is only among accepted headwords, within this corpus.
Data identified by its header as Unicode-DFS-2016; both that notice and the
current repository license are provided below.

HVULao Lao word segmentation dataset, v2.

https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/5zwym7kwn8/2
https://github.com/HaHVU/HVULao_NLP
